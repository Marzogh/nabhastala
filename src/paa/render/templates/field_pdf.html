REFERENCE

Using this field edition

Time and place

All displayed dates and times are local to {{ site_name }}. Overnight intervals begin on the printed date and may end after midnight on the following civil date.

Ratings

Text labels repeat every colour-coded rating for monochrome printing.

Low-Moon darkness

Intervals occur within astronomical darkness and apply the edition's Moon constraint. They are not interchangeable with Galactic Centre visibility, which additionally requires the core to clear the altitude threshold.

CSV tables

Full CSV tables for twilight, Moon, Galactic Centre, planets, satellites, minor planets, comets and events are available from the web data index.

Sources and scope

  • Solar, lunar, planetary and Galactic Centre circumstances are calculated for the named observing horizon.
  • Jupiter and Saturn satellite positions use NASA JPL Horizons.
  • Meteor and event sources are listed in the downloadable tables.
  • This PDF does not include live weather, cloud, smoke, local obstruction or artificial sky brightness.
